# Promotional series

Every scene of the story as its own video, with a gameplay screenshot of each fight and each walk through the city in between, in story order: 60 videos and 23 screenshots (each screenshot in two versions). The files are in `promo/videos/` (not in git) and sort in this order by name.

Videos: MP4, 1920×1080, 30 fps, with the game's music, voices and subtitles. Screenshots: PNG, 1920×1080; each comes with the HUD and without it ("clean").

Made with `tools/record-scenes.ts` and `tools/screenshot-fights.ts`; this list with `tools/promo-list.ts`.
