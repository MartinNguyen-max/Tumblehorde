# Campaign feedback, round 1 (2026-11-27)

The player played the campaign and asked for the changes below, plus a new storyline after Hrolf.
This is the work list; items are ticked as they land. Decisions made along the way go to `DECISIONS.md`.

Livia's assault by the Mongolians is part of the new story, but it is never shown: the scenes show
the city falling and the aftermath, and Livia tells Cassian later.
